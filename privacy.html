Moodling Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Moodling is a private, offline experience. It does not collect personal data.

Audio

Moodling uses the microphone only while you hold to release. Audio is processed locally in memory into abstract sound features and is immediately discarded. It is never recorded, saved, transcribed, uploaded, or shared.

Data stored on your device

Your lifeform, its evolution history, and your session counts are stored only on your device. This data is excluded from iCloud backup and from Android system backup and device-to-device transfer.

Moodling has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash-reporting SDK. It does not use the network.

Exports

If you choose to save or share an image, it is generated on your device and handed to the system Photos library or share sheet only at your request. Nothing is uploaded by Moodling.

Your choices

You can revoke microphone access at any time in the system Settings app. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data permanently.
